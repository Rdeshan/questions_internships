The term NewSQL was first coined in 2011 by Matthew Aslett, an analyst at 451 Research . NewSQL has evolved through three distinct phases 

Phase 1 (Early 2010s): Focused on in-memory processing for extreme speed.
Phase 2 (Mid 2010s): Focused on Distributed SQL, where databases like CockroachDB and TiDB mastered scaling across multiple data centers.
Phase 3 (Current 2026): Focused on AI-Native capabilities, where these databases now handle both standard data and the vector embeddings needed for AI agents
